3D MODELING PROMPT

Refine the existing simplified harbor into a charming, hand-crafted clay-like game model, preserving exactly one shared layout across all views. Use one cream timber-framed house with large softly rounded terracotta roof tiles and curved ridge caps, a gently sagging canvas awning on the right side wall, a broad wooden quay, a single finger pier projecting from its right end, one cargo hoist, one ground crate, one suspended cargo crate and two mooring bollards. Add thick rounded posts, rope collars, iron feet and collars, an open doorway with depth and timber knee braces. Do not add separate sheds, offices, barrels, additional buildings or scattered props.

Coordinate system: X along the shoreline, +Y landward, Z upward, metres. Quay footprint: X -6 to 6, Y 0 to 3.6; deck top Z 1.54. Finger footprint: X 3 to 6, Y -4.5 to 0, same deck height. House footprint: X -4.675 to -1.325, Y .95 to 3.35; eaves Z 4.16. Rounded roof tiles extend the roof slightly, with caps above ridge Z 5.65. The house door faces -Y (sea). The right-hand awning extends from X -1.27 to .35, Y .72 to 3.33. Hoist mast: X 5.55, Y -2.8. Hoist boom extends towards -X, never changes side. The ground crate stands at X -1.93, Y .43; the suspended crate is below the boom tip at X 2.89, Y -2.8. Two bollards stand at X 3.27 and 5.73, Y -4.16. The ladder is centered at X 4.45 on the seaward tip Y -4.62. Keep the open quay between the house and pier clear.

Use broad simple surfaces, visible rounded bevels, weighted normals, chunky proportions and matte honey wood, warm cream plaster, orange terracotta, pale canvas and subdued iron. Avoid sharp razor edges, realistic grain textures and thin engineering-like beams. Preserve readable structure rather than blobby geometry. Keep structural parts as separate named objects. No game terrain, water, boats, people or grid; the studio floor is a separate presentation object. Use the same geometry for front (-Y), back (+Y), right (+X) and top (+Z) orthographic views; do not recompose objects per view.

Simplify the attached harbor design into ONE coherent low-poly starter-port concept. Keep the broad wooden waterfront quay and the single projecting finger pier on the viewer's right. Keep ONLY ONE cream plaster, brown timber-framed harbor building with a warm orange simple gable roof, positioned on the rear LEFT half of the long quay so the projecting dock stays open. Keep one small simple cream canvas awning attached to the building's seaward facade, and one small timber cargo hoist on the outer right edge of the projecting pier. Remove both separate turquoise office and green warehouse. Remove all barrels, piles of crates, multiple lamps, spare sheds, repetitive clutter. Very simple buildable meshes, no photoreal texture, no tiny shingles: large flat roof panels, matte materials, restrained bevels, warm cozy low-poly appearance.
Render one isometric 3/4 design view on plain warm-light gray. Whole asset isolated, no ground terrain or water, no boats, no people, no grid. A single PORT ASSET, not a village. Keep building, platform and hoist easy to distinguish and suitable for recreating from primitive meshes in Blender.

Consistent four-view reference: one Blender scene rendered from four orthographic cameras, all with ortho_scale 15.0. The three-quarter beauty camera uses ortho_scale 16.7. build_reference.py reproduces the same styling pass. No game source was changed.
